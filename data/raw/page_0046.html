Mandatory Reporting Requirements for International Students

[Mandatory Reporting Requirements for International Students]

 

1. Address Change Notification

All international students must report any change of address to the immigration office immediately after moving.

Failure to report within 15 days of the move will result in a fine, so please be careful.

How to Report: Visit the Immigration Office or local Community Service Center, or submit online via HiKorea website(http://hikorea.go.kr)

Deadline: Within 15 days of moving

Required Documents: Copy of housing contract, Alien Registration Card

※ If your name is not on the housing contract, you must submit a Residence/Accommodation Confirmation Form signed by the person providing housing, along with a copy of their ID.


★ You must report even if your dormitory room has been changed.

 

2. Passport Change Notification

If you renew your passport due to expiration, or if any information such as your passport number or name changes,

you must report the change to the immigration office within 15 days of receiving the new passport.

Note: Your visa can only be extended up to your passport’s validity, so if your passport is about to expire, renew it before applying for a visa extension.

How to Report: Visit the Immigration Office or submit online via HiKorea website

Deadline: Within 15 days of passport issuance

Required Documents: Copy of new passport, Alien Registration Card, Application form

 

3. Part-Time Work Permission

International students must obtain permission from the immigration office before engaging in part-time work.

If a student works without permission, both the student and employer may be fined.

In serious cases, this may result in forced deportation and restrictions on future visa extensions.

Application Steps:

(1) Visit the International Cooperation Office (3rd floor, JBNU International Center) to prepare documents and get a signature from the officer in charge.

(2) Submit your application online at http://hikorea.go.kr (documents must be scanned)

When to Apply: At least 5 days before starting work

Eligibility: GPA of C (2.0) or above in the previous semester

Allowed Jobs: Simple labor or common part-time work for students (e.g., translation, interpretation, office assistant, restaurants)

Prohibited Jobs: Manufacturing, construction, teaching at academies, delivery, moving services, English camps/kids cafés, courier or food delivery services, etc.

Permitted Working Hours: Varies depending on course level, year, and language proficiency

4. Research Activities by Graduate Students

At JBNU (Jeonbuk National University):

If the university or LINC Foundation provides research allowances:

Research/internship related to your studies: Exempt from part-time work permission

Research/internship unrelated to your studies: Part-time work permission required

Outside JBNU:

If a non-university organization provides research funding:

Research/internship related to your studies: Part-time work permission required

Research/internship unrelated to your studies: Visa change to E-3 is required
