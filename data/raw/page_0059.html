This is JBNU International Affairs. 

We announce the undergraduate international student admission for 2025 Spring semester. 

Please see the attached admission guide for details of the admission procedures. 

 

■ Online Application site : https://www.uwayapply.com/

- You can use the website on application period only. 

- Visit http://uwayapply.com  - Click Int'l Student - Click (Jeonbuk National University - Undergraduate)

 

■ Self-introduction and study plan are not required. 

 

■ Document Submission (Complete the online application first)
