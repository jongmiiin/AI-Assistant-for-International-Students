[독일 마르부르크 대학] Intensive German Studies Program (IGSP)

마르부르크 대학에서 홍보하는 독일어 집중 프로그램 입니다.


관심있는 학생들은 아래쪽에 나왔있는 마르부르크 대학교 담당자에게 직접 연락하셔도 되고,

국제협력과 김원근 연구원 (outbound@jbnu.ac.kr / 063-270-4892)에게 연락주셔도 됩니다.


관심있는 학생들의 많은 참여 바랍니다. 이상입니다. 

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Marburg University is happy to announce that registration for our Intensive German Studies Program (IGSP) is now open!

IGSP is the perfect program for students of German, German Culture and German literature.

The program takes place from January 3rd until May 29th, 2026 and offers your students a chance to…

  • develop their German skills in daily, intensive language courses,
  • take a German literature course,
  • complete 298 contact hours,
  • immerse themselves into German life, culture and language,
  • enjoy excursions across Germany, including to Dresden and Berlin.

Included in the price are

  • room and meal subsidy,
  • free public transportation,
  • German medical insurance,
  • support and assistance from the International Office.

Cost: €7,700.00 (special conditions for students within bilateral institutional partnerships – contact us!))

 

If your students are interested in staying longer in Marburg, we also offer IUSP+Winter. This program combines the programs IGSP and IUSP (“International Undergraduate Studies Program”) to extend the time abroad until June 12th, 2026. In addition to the IGSP German language courses, students will…

  • integrate into the IUSP program, which includes an additional course on German Culture, Politics, and History,
  • have the chance to take courses from Marburg University’s regular course catalog,
  • earn up to 32 ECTS,
  • participate in the full cultural programs of IGSP and IUSP.

Cost: €9,850.00 (€7,550.00 for students from partner institutions)

 

For more details, please visit our program websites:

IGSP: https://www.uni-marburg.de/en/studying/studying-at-umr/exchange/igsp

IUSP+Winter: https://www.uni-marburg.de/en/studying/studying-at-umr/exchange/iusp/iusp

If you have any questions or students who may be interested in our programs, please let me know.
